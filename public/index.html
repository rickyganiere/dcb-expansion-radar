How to use the radar

This first working version is already usable without a backend.

1 · DiscoverSearchFind markets, operators, rails and ecosystem companies.
2 · PrioritizeShortlistSave the markets you want to work. Stored locally on this device.
3 · DecideCompareCompare up to three live markets and their verified billing routes.
4 · ActOutreachGenerate and copy a first-touch commercial email for a target company.
Current data is evidence-backed but manually curated. The next backend phase will move the same schema into Supabase and add automated discovery, change detection, contact enrichment and shared team persistence.
